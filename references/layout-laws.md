# Layout Laws

These are working hypotheses for critique. They are not universal formulas. Use them to explain an observed relationship, then verify against the user's goal and the image.

| Law | Look for | Helpful when | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| 三分法 | Subject, title, or horizon sits near a thirds line or intersection | A clear focal hierarchy is needed without dead centering | Mechanical placement with no semantic reason |
| 黄金比例 / 螺旋 | Scale and spacing create a gradual path toward the focal point | A calm, guided reading path is desired | False precision; content does not follow the path |
| 对角线 | Major edges or type baselines create a directional vector | Energy, movement, or tension matters | Decorative tilt without a stable anchor |
| 负空间 | Empty field isolates, frames, or points toward a subject | Premium, quiet, or high-contrast communication | Empty area has no role and weakens information density |
| 居中 / 对称 | Mass, axes, and margins mirror around a center | Authority, ceremony, clarity, or stillness | Static result when the message needs motion |
| 偏心 / 非对称平衡 | Unequal masses are balanced by scale, contrast, or whitespace | Editorial, contemporary, or expressive layouts | One side becomes visually disposable |
| 引导线 / 汇聚线 | Lines, edges, gaze, or perspective converge on a target | Fast scan and strong focal direction | Lines compete with the message |
| 奇数法则 / 组群 | Three or five elements form a readable unit | Product groups, cards, or repeated motifs | Arbitrary counting without grouping logic |
| 框中框 | Borders, windows, or nested shapes isolate a layer | Layered storytelling or strong framing | Too many containers flatten hierarchy |
| 网格 | Repeated columns, rows, baselines, and shared edges | Dense copy, editorial work, and system consistency | Grid becomes visible as a cage |
| 比例对比 | Title, support text, image, and margin have intentional scale ratios | Quick hierarchy and legibility | Large type is used as a substitute for hierarchy |
| 填满 / 破格 | Content intentionally reaches or crosses the frame | Impact, urgency, or tactile density | Crowding and loss of breathing room |

## Evidence Language

Prefer statements such as:

- "标题与主体图片的左边缘没有共享锚点，导致首屏扫描分成两条路径。"
- "右上区域的高对比小块抵消了主体重量，但目前离边界过近，像裁切错误而不是有意的偏心。"
- "如果目标是安静和高级，可把这块留白变成框景；如果目标是促销，则应减少留白并强化价格层级。"

Avoid statements such as "看起来不够高级" without naming the relationship that causes the impression.
